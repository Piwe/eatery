Ouma, Nonna & Teta

One table, three kitchens

Comfort is
an inherited recipe.

Three grandmothers, three kitchens, one long table. Ouma cooks the food of Durban and the Cape. Teta cooks the Levant and the islands. Nonna cooks Naples. Nobody here has ever agreed on which one is best, and that is the whole point.

Three kitchens

  • Ouma

    Durban curry and Cape spice. Bread as a bowl, mielie meal as a bed, and a bay leaf on top of everything baked. Feeds nine when six were expected.

  • Teta

    The Levant and the islands. Everything arrives at once, in small dishes, and the bread is not optional. Olive oil is a seasoning, not a cooking fat.

  • Nonna

    Naples by way of a very small kitchen. Four ingredients, done properly, and a standing argument about how long the ragù has been on.

Ouma’s kitchen Durban & the Cape

  • A quarter loaf hollowed out and filled with mutton curry. The bread you dig out goes on top. Durban, 1940s, invented for people who needed lunch they could carry. Halaal

  • Stiff white maize meal, a hot bean-and-pepper relish, and a coil of farm sausage off the fire. The plate everyone at a braai builds for themselves.

  • Curried mince baked under a set egg custard, sweetened with fruit, with bay leaves standing up out of the top. Cape Malay cooking — itself already three cuisines deep.

  • Apricot jam sponge, still hot, drowned in cream the moment it leaves the oven. Served with custard because Ouma does not accept the question. Vegetarian

Teta’s kitchen the Levant & the islands

  • Eggs poached in a pan of tomato, pepper and cumin, brought to the table in the pan it was cooked in. Bread, not cutlery. VegetarianHalaal

  • Aubergine, lamb and cinnamon under a lid of béchamel, browned hard at the edges. Cut it too early and it collapses; everyone cuts it too early.

  • A wide swirl of hummus with a well of olive oil in the middle, paprika across it, and halloumi off the griddle while it still squeaks. VegetarianHalaal

Nonna’s kitchen Naples

  • Pasta, pecorino, black pepper, pasta water. Four things. The entire dish is temperature control and nerve. Vegetarian

  • Ragù that started on Saturday morning, béchamel, and a corner piece that people are prepared to fall out over.

  • Saffron, butter, and twenty minutes of not walking away from the pan. Loose enough to move when you tap the plate. Vegetarian

  • Savoiardi soaked exactly as long as they should be, mascarpone, and cocoa dusted over the top at the last second so it stays matte. Vegetarian
